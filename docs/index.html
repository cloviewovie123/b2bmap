      About

      Bin to Better collects used tennis balls from clubs and academies and passes them on for reuse and recycling. This map shows every collection site we pick up from and every dropoff site that received balls.

      How to read it

      green circles are collection sites, coral circles are dropoff sites. A bigger circle means more balls. Click a circle for its visit history.

      A dashed outline means the exact location isn't known, so the pin is approximate.

      The data

      The route comparison uses real driving times from OpenStreetMap data and Google OR-Tools. Pick any stops in the Plan tab; it assumes one driver starting and ending in Fremont.
